Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how JEH Electronics ("we," "us," or "our"), the parent organization of Hatmaker App, collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile applications (the "Apps") on the Google Play Store.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

For our initial suite of games (including Hangman, Mastermind, and Battleship), we do not collect, store, or share any personal information. These apps are designed to be "privacy-first," meaning:

  • No account creation is required.
  • No contact info (names, emails, phone numbers) is gathered.
  • No precise location tracking is used.

2. Technical Data & Analytics

We may use basic, non-identifying technical data (such as device model, OS version, and crash reports) provided by Google Play Services to improve app performance. This data is anonymized and cannot be used to identify you personally.

3. Third-Party Services

Our Apps are distributed via the Google Play Store. Google may collect data according to their own Privacy Policy. We recommend reviewing the Google Privacy Policy for more information on how they handle your data.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect personal user data or require accounts, there is no personal data stored on our servers to delete. If this changes in future updates, we will provide a clear web-based mechanism for data deletion as required by Google Play policy.

5. Children's Privacy

Our games are intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such info, please contact us immediately.
